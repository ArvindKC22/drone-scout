About this simulator

Drone Scout Flight Lab implements the survey-planning, point-mass flight, battery and coverage equations from the Drone Scout Technical Thesis Report in the browser.

  • Real vs simulated: map rasters here are generated procedurally; the report used historical orthomosaic derivatives. Aircraft motion, battery, wind, GNSS and capture timing are simulated.
  • Not a digital twin. Power and controller constants are uncalibrated assumptions, not flight-log fits. Do not use for flight authorisation.
  • Coverage is a footprint-hit estimate on a 0.5 m grid, not image-overlap or photogrammetric quality.
  • Random draws use a JS PRNG, so numbers differ slightly from the Python package for the same seed.
